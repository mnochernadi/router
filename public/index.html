Testing walks the list in batches through a throwaway xray instance (one inbound per candidate), records latency + exit IP, marks the best for the live pool and restarts xray with them. An exit that answers a rate limit is burned and leaves the pool; once it drains, the burn pile is deleted, new candidates are tested and the pool refills itself. Accounts

OpenCode's free tier refuses every non-OpenCode client (403 FreeTierError) — verified from this server across 7 header sets and both endpoints. It cannot be fixed with a proxy or an account.

Sign in — a principal, not another identity

The anonymous allowance is one shared daily bucket: addresses, identities, builds, sessions, models and paths were all measured against it and none of them move it. A signed-in account is a different bucket, so each sign-in adds an allowance and the ladder rotates across them.
